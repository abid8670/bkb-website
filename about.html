Company Introduction

A Forward-Thinking Development Company.

Established in 2019, Bin Kamal Builders is a forward-thinking real estate development company committed to creating distinctive spaces that bring together contemporary architecture, thoughtful planning, and modern lifestyles.

Driven by a commitment to quality and excellence, the company focuses on developing residential destinations that offer comfort, functionality, and lasting value. Every project is approached with careful attention to design, construction, and the evolving needs of modern communities.

With a vision to create spaces that inspire better living, Bin Kamal Builders continues to pursue developments that combine refined design, practical living, and enduring investment potential.

Our Subsidiary

DevPro Contractors & Engineers

DevPro Contractors & Engineers is the construction subsidiary of Bin Kamal Builders — a PEC-registered firm delivering residential, commercial, grey structure and finishing works across Lahore and Islamabad.

Our mission is simple: make construction, renovation and building projects easy, efficient and stress-free. From a private residence to a commercial plaza — we handle it all under one roof, one contract, one team, backed by qualified engineers and transparent reporting.
