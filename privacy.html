Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how Bin Kamal Builders (“BKB”, “we”, “us”) handles information collected through this website. By using this website and submitting an enquiry, you consent to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry form, request details, or download a brochure, we may collect the information you provide — such as your name, phone number, email address, city and the nature of your enquiry. We may also collect basic technical information that your browser sends automatically, such as device type and pages visited.

How We Use Your Information

Information you provide is used to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and share project, pricing and payment-plan details;
  • Contact you by phone, WhatsApp or email regarding your enquiry;
  • Improve our website, services and communication;
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with our internal sales and partnerships teams, or with service providers who help us operate this website and communicate with you. Any such parties are expected to handle your information responsibly.

Cookies & Third-Party Services

This website may use cookies and similar technologies to support basic functionality and analyse usage. Some pages embed third-party services such as Google Maps, which may set their own cookies and are governed by their own privacy policies. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you provide. We retain enquiry information only for as long as necessary to respond to you and for our legitimate business and legal purposes.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you have provided, or ask us to stop contacting you, by emailing Binkamalbuilders@gmail.com or calling 0332-4448445.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
